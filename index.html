30 Days of Three.js

A personal challenge. 30 days of learning Three.js, building one 3D scene a day in the browser. This page tracks my progress as I work through shapes, lighting, textures, movement and effects.

3 of 30 completed

  • Day 01: Spinning Shape with a Moving Light

    A twenty sided shape floating in the dark, with a thin outline drawn over it. A light circles around it while you watch, so the bright side keeps moving. You can drag to turn the shape and scroll to move closer or further away.

  • Day 02: The Earth with Clouds and City Lights

    The Earth, tilted the same way the real one is, built from a few see through layers stacked on top of each other. There is a photo of the surface, city lights that switch on across the night side, clouds drifting slowly over the top, and a soft blue glow around the edge. Thousands of stars sit behind it.

  • Day 03: Flying Through a Tunnel

    A ride through a glowing tunnel that loops back on itself, so it never ends. The camera follows a curved path with a light attached to it, lighting up the walls as it goes. Small shapes float along the way and fade out just as you reach them.
