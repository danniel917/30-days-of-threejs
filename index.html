30 Days of Three.js

A personal challenge — 30 days of learning Three.js, one WebGL scene at a time. This page tracks my progress as I work through geometry, lighting, materials, animation and shaders.

3 of 30 completed

  • Day 01 — Icosahedron with Spotlight

    A flat-shaded icosahedron with a translucent wireframe overlay, lit by an animated spotlight that orbits the shape while a hemisphere light fills the shadows. Includes orbit controls with clamped zoom.

  • Day 02 — Earth with Clouds and City Lights

    Four layered shells on a 23.4° tilted axis — an 8k day map with a normal map for terrain relief, additive night-side city lights, a cloud layer drifting slightly ahead of the surface, and a Fresnel shader for the atmospheric rim glow, set against a 5000-star field.

  • Day 03 — Tunnel Flythrough

    A closed Catmull-Rom spline extruded into a tube, flown through from the inside — the camera rides the curve with getPointAt and aims at a point further along it. The wireframe is built by hand from the tube’s vertex grid so only the rings and lengthwise lines draw, a spotlight parented to the camera lights the way, and random polyhedra scattered along the path spin and fade out as they pass.
